Class 1B Swimming lessons begin: Thursday, 15 October 2026 · PE Teacher: Mr Christos Savva

Dear Parents and Guardians,

As part of our Physical Education curriculum, swimming lessons for Class 1B will begin on Thursday, 15 October 2026, at the school Swimming Pool during the class's scheduled PE periods.

Swimming equipment: On swimming days, your child should bring a swimming costume, swimming cap, goggles, towel, bathrobe/swimming robe, shower gel and shampoo for use after the lesson, as well as suitable footwear for the pool area. Please ensure that all items are clearly labelled with your child's name. After each lesson, students will have adequate time to shower, dry, change and then continue with the rest of their normal school timetable.

Weather conditions & PE kit: please also ensure your child brings their usual PE kit on swimming days. Physical Education will take place regardless of the weather. If it is raining, the lesson will still go ahead in an adapted form, held in a suitable indoor or covered area. If conditions are too cold or otherwise unsuitable for swimming, the swimming session will be postponed and rescheduled for a more appropriate time, and the class will complete an alternative PE lesson in the meantime.

Please be assured that the school follows all appropriate health and safety procedures; the pool conditions and water temperature are properly maintained and monitored; and every lesson is supervised by qualified PE staff. If your child cannot participate in swimming for any medical reason, please inform the PE Department in writing in advance.

Yours sincerely,
The Physical Education Department - PASCAL Private Secondary School Lemesos
