# District Mathematics Competition 2026–2027

**От:** STEPHANOU ANDREAS · **Кому:** URVANTSEV MAKAR · **Дата:** 2026-10-03 18:24:14

DISTRICT MATHEMATICS COMPETITION 2026–2027

We would like to inform all interested students that registration for participation in the District Mathematics Competition for the school year 2026–2027 is now open.

Registration can be completed exclusively online through the registration portal of the Cyprus Mathematical Society.

Date and Time: Saturday, 7 November 2026, 10:00–12:00

Examination Centres:
- Nicosia: Latsia Lyceum
- Limassol: Lanitio Lyceum
- Larnaca: Vergina Lyceum
- Famagusta: Paralimni Lyceum
- Paphos: A' Ethnarch Makarios III Lyceum

Eligibility: students in Grades 5 and 6 of Primary School, and students in all grades of Gymnasium and Lyceum.

Registration Deadline: Friday, 23 October 2026.

All students who participate in the competition will receive a Certificate of Participation.

Important Information: Participation is permitted only for students who have completed their registration within the deadline and are included on the official list of participants with the candidate code sent to them. Students should remember their candidate code and bring it with them, as the participation lists will be published using these candidate codes.
