# Aristidou Elena - History introduction and expectations

**От:** ARISTIDOU ELENA · **Кому:** URVANTSEV MAKAR · **Дата:** 2026-09-19 18:46:40

Dear Parents and Guardians,

I hope you are all well.

My name is Elena Aristidou and I am the Year 1 History teacher. It was lovely to meet the students during our first introductory History lesson, where we explored what History is and what they can look forward to learning this year.

I would like to highlight a few important expectations for our History lessons. Regular participation and homework are an important part of the course and contribute 40% of the semester grade, while tests contribute 60%.

Please encourage your child to:
1. Bring their History book or printed copies to every lesson.
2. Complete and submit homework on time.
3. Bring a notebook for History.
4. Keep a plastic folder for handouts and other materials.

If a student forgets their book or fails to complete their homework on three occasions, I will contact you so that we can work together to support them.

Please find attached the handout given to students during our first lesson, which provides an introduction to History.

Thank you for your support and cooperation. I look forward to working with you to make History an enjoyable and successful subject for your child.

Warm regards,

Elena Aristidou
Year 1 History Teacher
