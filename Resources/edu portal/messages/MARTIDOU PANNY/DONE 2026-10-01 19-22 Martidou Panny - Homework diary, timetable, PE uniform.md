Dear All Year 1 Parents,

Please make sure children have a homework notebook or a homework diary. I would like you to sign the homework notebook diary acknowledging that you have seen what homework they have been assigned and that they have completed it. We will be checking during form period to see that they've had it signed.

Additionally, I recommend your children have their Class Timetable (Schedule), Pupils' Calendar and Semester A Test Calendar posted where they sit to do homework so they can be better organised. I've already told them to pack their school bag from the night before by looking at their Class Timetable so they pack what they need for following day at school.

Also, make sure that they bring their school uniform on the day they have PE because they need to change out of their PE clothes and wear their white polo shirt, shorts, pants, skort or skirt. PE clothes are worn ONLY for PE!

We need to work together to make sure our children are becoming more responsible. Let's keep in mind that this is a huge transition from primary school (see attached).Thank you.
