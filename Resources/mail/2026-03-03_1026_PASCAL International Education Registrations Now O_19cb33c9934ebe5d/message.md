Thank you for your interest in PASCAL International Education Lemesos.

We are pleased to inform you that registrations for the 2026/27 academic year are now open. To secure your child’s place, we kindly request the following:

For Kindergarten & Primary School Year 1 (ages: 3-6):

- Completed Registration Form of Kindergarten (available here) or Primary (available here) and return it to our school office. If you are abroad, please send the completed form at admissionslem@pascal.ac.cy. Once you are in Cyprus, you are kindly requested to visit our school office to sign the documents in person as well.
- Provide the original passports for both parents and the child. Again, if you are not in Cyprus, please send copies via email.
- A Registration Fee of €3,000 is also required. This includes €750 registration fee, €50 Parents/Guardians Association subscription, and €2,200 as a down payment.

Payments can be made via credit card, JCC Smart, wire transfer, standing order, or cash at the school office. For more details, please refer to the Tuition Fees for the Main Campus (available here) or the City Centre Campus (available here).

The Main Campus office is located at 2, Grammaton Street 3112 Agia Fyla, Lemesos (open from 8:00-15:00)

The City Centre campus temporary office is at 107, Yianni Kranidioti Avenue 3112, Lemesos (open from 8:00-15:00)

For Primary School Years 2-6 (ages: 7-12):

- Please provide your children's last year’s school report and any SEN (Special Educational Needs) report if applicable. After reviewing the reports, we will invite you for an assessment (if required).

If you would prefer a meeting to discuss your children's registration, tour our facilities or discuss any other matter, please do not hesitate to contact the Lemesos Admissions Office (admissionslem@pascal.ac.cy) or at +357 22 509 240 (for main campus) or +357 22509570 (for city centre campus).
