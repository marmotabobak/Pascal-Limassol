**Uniform Regulations (summary)**
- **Daily:** Polo shirt, trousers/shorts (boys) or trousers/skirt (girls), black shoes, black belt, coloured socks.

- **Excursions:** Daily uniform for educational; jeans + plain t-shirt for non-educational.
